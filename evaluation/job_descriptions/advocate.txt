Advocate / Legal Associate
A law firm is hiring an Advocate to handle litigation and advisory work.
Responsibilities: draft plaints, petitions, written statements, affidavits and legal notices; appear before district courts, tribunals and the High Court; conduct legal research on case law and statutes; draft and vet contracts and agreements; advise clients on civil, criminal, property and corporate matters.
Requirements: LLB and enrolment with the Bar Council; strong drafting and argument skills; knowledge of civil and criminal procedure and the law of evidence.
