HR Executive
We are looking for an HR Executive to manage the full employee lifecycle.
Responsibilities: end-to-end recruitment including sourcing, screening and scheduling interviews; onboarding and induction of new joiners; maintaining employee records and HR policies; payroll and attendance coordination; employee engagement activities; handling grievances and exit formalities; statutory compliance such as PF and ESI.
Requirements: MBA or degree in Human Resources; knowledge of labour laws; HRMS tools and MS Excel; excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
