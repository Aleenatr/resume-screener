Fitness Trainer / Nutrition Coach
A fitness centre is hiring a certified trainer.
Responsibilities: design personalised workout and diet plans; conduct personal training and group classes in strength, cardio, yoga and aerobics; assess clients' fitness, body composition and progress; advise on nutrition, weight management and a healthy lifestyle; ensure safe use of gym equipment.
Requirements: certification in fitness training, sports science or nutrition and dietetics; knowledge of exercise physiology, anatomy and first aid; strong motivation and communication skills.
