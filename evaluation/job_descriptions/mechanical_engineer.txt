Mechanical Engineer
A manufacturing company is hiring a Mechanical Engineer for design and production.
Responsibilities: design mechanical components and assemblies in AutoCAD, SolidWorks or CATIA; prepare drawings and bills of material; support production, maintenance and quality on the shop floor; conduct root cause analysis and preventive maintenance; work on process improvement and cost reduction.
Requirements: B.E. or B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering; knowledge of manufacturing processes, machine design and thermodynamics; CAD tools; familiarity with ISO quality standards.
