Civil Engineer
A construction company is hiring a Site Civil Engineer.
Responsibilities: supervise construction of buildings, roads and structures; execute work as per drawings and specifications; prepare estimates, BOQ and bar bending schedules; quantity surveying and billing; quality control of concrete and materials; coordinate with contractors and ensure site safety.
Requirements: B.E. or Diploma in Civil Engineering; AutoCAD, STAAD Pro and MS Project; knowledge of IS codes, surveying and structural design.
