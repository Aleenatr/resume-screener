Arts and Culture Professional
We are looking for an arts graduate to support our creative and educational programmes.
Responsibilities: plan and teach art, music, dance or drama sessions; organise exhibitions, cultural events and workshops; create artwork, illustrations or performances; write content and coordinate with schools and community groups.
Requirements: BA, BFA or MA in Fine Arts, Performing Arts or Humanities; creativity and presentation skills; experience with painting, drawing, music or theatre; good communication skills.
